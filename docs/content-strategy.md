## Topic clusters

### Website management

Pillar: `/website-management`. Support it with maintenance checklists, cost and scope guides, redesign comparisons, management warning signs and monthly-support explainers. Link supporting articles to maintenance and WordPress service pages where relevant.

### SEO

Pillar: `/seo`. Support it with local and technical checklists, time-to-result explanations, indexation diagnosis and SEO-versus-ads comparisons. Avoid fixed ranking promises and generic location pages.

### Paid growth

Pillar: `/paid-growth`. Support it with cost-factor guides, channel comparisons, landing-page readiness and conversion-measurement guidance. Separate media spend from management fees in every relevant article.

### Conversion and analytics

Pillars: `/conversion-rate-optimisation` and `/analytics-conversion-tracking`. Explain form measurement, meaningful events, consent limitations, service-page clarity and evidence-led testing.

### AI and automation

Pillar: `/ai-workflow-automation`. Cover realistic workflow examples, data quality, privacy, approval controls, tool limitations and discovery. Avoid presenting autonomous decision-making as the default.

## Publishing rules

- Improve existing articles before increasing volume.
- Store incomplete or unverified material as drafts; do not publish it automatically.
- Add original examples only when the business can substantiate them.
- Cite current, authoritative sources for external facts.
- Use a descriptive title, one H1, useful H2/H3 hierarchy, direct opening answer and a next step.
- Link from each article to one primary service page and two genuinely related resources.
- Review content after material service, platform or regulatory changes.

## Internal linking priorities

- Homepage → service overview → dedicated service pages.
- Dedicated services → closely related specialist services and relevant articles.
- Articles → one primary service and related articles.
- Work examples → website management and contact paths.
- About → approved work and enquiry routes.
- Review and contact pages → privacy policy and relevant services.
