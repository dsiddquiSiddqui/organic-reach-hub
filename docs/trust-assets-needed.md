# Trust assets and verified business information needed

The website deliberately avoids filling these gaps with invented content.

## Founder and business identity

- Founder/co-owner full name and preferred public role.
- Approved professional biography, including which parts of the approximately ten years of experience may be stated publicly.
- Genuine founder portrait with publication permission and alt-text guidance.
- LinkedIn profile or other verified professional profile, if it should be linked.
- Legal business name and any registration information that must appear publicly.
- Confirmed public email, telephone number, postal address and service territories, if intended for publication.

## Portfolio and proof

- Original-resolution project screenshots to replace PDF-derived crops where available.
- Written publication permission for any future client names, logos, screenshots or quotations.
- Per-project scope, dates, platforms and deliverables supported by project records.
- Verified outcomes with their measurement source, period, baseline and attribution limitations.
- Current Organic Reach HUB client examples, only where the relationship and permission are confirmed.
- Genuine testimonials with reviewer identity, role, business, source, date and written reuse permission.

## Commercial and operational detail

- Package pricing, only if the business chooses to publish it.
- Contract notice, handover, reporting, support-hours and emergency-response terms.
- Precise free-review deliverable and any response-time commitment the business can consistently meet.
- Final analytics and consent configuration.
- Production email or notification workflow for new Supabase leads, if required.

## Publication controls

Portfolio records must remain unpublished unless `verified`, `permissionStatus: 'approved'`, `publishStatus: 'published'` and at least one approved public asset are all present. Metrics should remain empty until evidence and permission are recorded.
